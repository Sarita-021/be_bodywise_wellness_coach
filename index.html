A simple review routine

Use these checkpoints to record your own experience.

1
Before starting

Read the label, directions and warnings. Record a baseline and what you hope to learn.

2
During early use

Follow directions and note unwanted reactions. Do not increase dosage to speed up results.

3
Review over time

Compare notes and photos taken in similar conditions. Changes may have multiple causes and do not prove product efficacy.

4
Know when to seek help

For concerning reactions, stop use where appropriate and seek qualified advice. Persistent hair loss or skin symptoms merit professional assessment.
